Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Kintsugi is a personal tool that uploads music videos to its developer's own YouTube channel. It has no other users and no servers of its own.

What it accesses

When you sign in with Google, Kintsugi asks for two YouTube permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload the videos it makes and set their thumbnails.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): only to show the name of the channel that uploads will go to.

It does not read, change or delete your other videos, comments, playlists or account data.

What it stores

The sign-in token Google issues is saved only in a file on the computer where Kintsugi runs. It is never sent anywhere except to Google, to make the requests above. Kintsugi collects no analytics and keeps no other data about you.

Sharing

Kintsugi shares no data with anyone. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can remove Kintsugi's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
